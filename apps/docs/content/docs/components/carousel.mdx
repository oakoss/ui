## Usage

```tsx
import {
  Carousel,
  CarouselContent,
  CarouselItem,
  CarouselNext,
  CarouselPrevious,
} from '#/components/ui/layout/carousel';

<Carousel aria-label="Featured products">
  <CarouselContent>
    <CarouselItem>…</CarouselItem>
    <CarouselItem>…</CarouselItem>
    <CarouselItem>…</CarouselItem>
  </CarouselContent>
  <CarouselPrevious />
  <CarouselNext />
</Carousel>;
```

The buttons sit 3rem outside the slides, so leave that room beside the
carousel, or place them with `className`. `opts` passes options to
[Embla](https://www.embla-carousel.com/api/options/), such as `loop`,
`align`, `dragFree` and `breakpoints`, and `plugins` takes Embla plugins.

## Examples

### Sizes

A slide's `basis-*` sets how many show at once, and responsive classes
change that by width. `opts={{ slidesToScroll: 'auto' }}` steps a view at a
time rather than a slide.

<ComponentPreview name="carousel-sizes">
  <CarouselSizes />
</ComponentPreview>

### Loop and peek

`opts={{ loop: true }}` wraps from the last slide to the first, so neither
button disables. A basis under full width, such as `basis-4/5`, lets the
next slide peek in; `align: 'start'` lines the current slide up with the
start edge.

<ComponentPreview name="carousel-loop">
  <CarouselLoop />
</ComponentPreview>

### Vertical

`orientation="vertical"` scrolls up and down; give `CarouselContent` a
height. The buttons sit above and below.

</ComponentPreview>

### Counter

`setApi` hands you Embla's API, for controls and readouts of your own, such
as a visible count. The carousel already announces each slide, so a
counter like this one is hidden from screen readers.

## Accessibility

The carousel follows the
[carousel pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/carousel/): a
`region` with the role description "carousel", whose name is required
(`aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`), holding slides that are `group`s with
the role description "slide", each named by its position ("2 of 5").

This component adds:

- **Hidden slides:** slides out of view are `inert`, so they leave the tab
  order and the accessibility tree; a slide keeps focus it already holds as
  it scrolls away, until focus leaves it.
- **Announcements:** once the carousel moves and comes to rest, a `status`
  region names the slides at least half on screen ("Slide 2 of 5", "Slides
  4 to 6 of 9"); nothing is announced on load or mid-drag.
- **Focus at the ends:** when Previous or Next disables at the first or last
  slide, focus moves to the other button rather than to the page.
- **Reduced motion:** with `prefers-reduced-motion`, the buttons jump
  between slides rather than animating; a drag still follows the pointer.
- **Clicks after a drag:** releasing a drag over a link or button in a slide
  doesn't activate it, which Embla handles.
- **Nested carousels:** a drag that starts in a carousel inside a slide moves
  only that one, unless `opts` sets its own `watchDrag`.
- **Visibility:** the buttons keep their border and focus outline in forced
  colors.

The carousel takes no arrow keys, since the pattern defines none, so text
fields and other controls in a slide keep theirs.

| Key               | Action                                                    |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Tab`             | Moves through the buttons and the content of slides shown |
| `Enter` / `Space` | Shows the previous or next slide from its button          |

## API

### Carousel

A `div` with Embla's carousel. Its strings, from the role descriptions to
the announcement, can be replaced through `messages`.

#### Props

<auto-type-table
  path="../../../../../packages/ui/src/components/ui/layout/carousel.tsx"
  name="CarouselProps"
/>

Also accepts `div` props.

### CarouselContent

The viewport and the row of slides, as `div`s; its `className` styles the
row.

### CarouselItem

A slide, as a `div`. Size it with `basis-*`.

### CarouselPrevious and CarouselNext

Extend [Button](/docs/components/button), outline and icon-sized by default.
Their names come from `messages`, or their own `aria-label`.

#### Data attributes

| Attribute   | Present when                                                                                    |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `data-slot` | Always: `carousel`, `carousel-content`, `carousel-item`, `carousel-previous` or `carousel-next` |
| `inert`     | On a `carousel-item` out of view that doesn't hold focus                                        |

### useCarousel

Reads the carousel's state inside it, for parts of your own: the slides in
view, whether each button can move, and the `go` function the buttons call.
