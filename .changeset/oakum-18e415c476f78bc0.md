Chart legends and locale ticks work without wiring: ChartPlot fills the legend's colors and dimming, ChartContainer holds the hidden series (hiddenSeries, defaultHiddenSeries, onHiddenSeriesChange) and a shared number and date format, and numeric axes format their ticks in the app's locale. ChartLegend is interactive by default (isStatic for a list) and drops its hidden and onHiddenChange props: move them to ChartContainer as hiddenSeries and onHiddenSeriesChange, and drop color and states from definitions that only set them for the legend. A definition that keeps its own color passes useChart().hidden to chartColor, or its legend takes isStatic.
